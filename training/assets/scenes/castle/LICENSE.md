# Castle scene asset

"Skokloster Castle" hall scan, distributed with the Habitat test scenes
(https://dl.fbaipublicfiles.com/habitat/habitat-test-scenes_v1.0.zip),
original photogrammetry published on Sketchfab by the Swedish National
Historical Museums, licensed CC Attribution (commercial use permitted
with attribution).

Only used locally for simulation; the raw/processed asset files are
gitignored and re-created by `./run.sh room-assets --zip-member
skokloster-castle.glb --name castle`.
